Privacy

DestinyOracle is built for a global audience. We collect as little as possible.

Birth details you enter are processed in your browser to generate a deterministic reading. We do not operate a server that stores your name, birth date, place, or questions.

Past readings and streak data are saved in your device’s localStorage only. Clearing site data removes them. Share links encode reading inputs in the URL — treat them like a postcard anyone with the link can open.

This product is entertainment. It is not medical, legal, or financial advice.
